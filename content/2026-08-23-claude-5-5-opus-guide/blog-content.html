Tips and Tricks for Maximum Efficiency

To truly get the most out of Claude 5.5 Opus, you need to refine your approach to prompting and interaction. Here are some advanced strategies to elevate your results:

  • Use XML Tags for Structure: Claude responds exceptionally well when inputs are organized using XML tags (e.g., <context>, <instructions>). This helps the model parse complex prompts accurately.
  • Implement Chain-of-Thought Prompting: Ask the model to "think step-by-step" before providing an answer. This significantly improves its performance on logic and math problems.
