Battery Life and Charging Speeds

Battery anxiety is a real thing, but the iPhone 18 Pro leaks bring good news. Thanks to the efficiency of the A20 Pro chip and a new stacked battery technology, we could see a significant jump in battery life. Stacked batteries allow for a higher energy density in the same physical footprint, meaning Apple can pack more power into the sleek chassis. Rumors suggest you could easily get through two days of moderate use on a single charge. For heavy users, it means you can finally leave your power bank at home.

Charging speeds are also reportedly getting a much-needed bump. While Apple has traditionally been conservative with fast charging to preserve battery health, the iPhone 18 Pro is rumored to support up to 45W wired charging and faster 25W MagSafe wireless charging. This means you could charge your phone from 0 to 50% in just under 20 minutes. For those moments when you are rushing out the door and realize your battery is dead, this faster charging capability will be an absolute lifesaver.

Furthermore, reverse wireless charging might finally make an appearance. Imagine being able to charge your AirPods or Apple Watch simply by placing them on the back of your iPhone 18 Pro. This feature has been requested by users for years, and leaks indicate Apple has finally perfected the technology to make it efficient and reliable. It is these small quality-of-life improvements that truly enhance the user experience.
