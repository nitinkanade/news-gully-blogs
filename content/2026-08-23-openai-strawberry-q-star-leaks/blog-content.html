OpenAI Strawberry vs. GPT-4

To truly appreciate the advancements detailed in the OpenAI Strawberry Q* leaks, it is helpful to compare it directly with the current gold standard: GPT-4. While GPT-4 and its iterative upgrades (like GPT-4o) are incredibly powerful, they still operate under fundamental limitations that Strawberry aims to overcome.

GPT-4 is fundamentally a statistical engine. It predicts the next most likely word based on its vast training data. This makes it excellent at writing essays, generating creative content, and answering factual questions that exist in its dataset. However, when faced with a novel logic puzzle or a multi-step math problem it hasn't seen before, it often stumbles. It lacks an internal "thinking space."

Strawberry, on the other hand, utilizes a technique often referred to in research circles as "Test-Time Compute" or "System 2 thinking." When you ask GPT-4 a question, it generates the answer almost instantly. When you ask Strawberry a complex question, it might take 10, 20, or even 60 seconds to respond. During this time, it is generating multiple internal reasoning paths, evaluating them, and selecting the most logical outcome before generating the final text for you.
