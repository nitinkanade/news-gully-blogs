What is the Jio Google Gemini Pro Offer?

Who is Eligible for the 18-Month Free Access?

To take advantage of this special promotion, you must satisfy the following criteria:

  • Active Jio 5G Unlimited Prepaid or Postpaid Plan: Plans starting from ₹349 and above (or annual plans).
  • Jio True 5G Welcome Offer Enabled: Your handset must be 5G-ready and connected to Jio's True 5G network.
  • MyJio App Installed: Updated to the latest version available on Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
  • Active Google Account: A personal Gmail account not currently tied to another conflicting Google One family subscription.

Step-by-Step Activation Guide via MyJio App

Activating your subscription takes less than two minutes. Follow these quick steps:

  1. Open the MyJio App on your 5G smartphone.
  2. Scroll to the Trending Offers banner on the home dashboard.
  3. Tap on "Claim 18 Months Google Gemini Pro Offer".
  4. Click Activate Now and select the Google Account you wish to link.
  5. Confirm the authorization permissions on Google's secure portal.
  6. Once verified, your Google One and Gemini Advanced dashboard will show Active Subscription: 18 Months Courtesy of Jio.
💡 Pro Tip: Make sure your MyJio app is updated to the latest build before claiming. If the banner doesn't appear immediately, go to MyJio > Coupons & Winnings to manually trigger your voucher code.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is credit card required to claim the Jio Google Gemini Pro offer?

No credit card or payment information is required. The offer authenticates directly via your active Jio 5G phone number inside the MyJio application.

What happens after the 18 months free period expires?

After 18 months, the plan will revert to the standard free tier unless you manually choose to renew. You will not be auto-charged.

Can I share the 2TB Google One storage with my family?

Yes, Google One allows you to share your 2TB storage quota with up to 5 family members at no additional fee.
